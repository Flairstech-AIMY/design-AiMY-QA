Product Insights, cases sheet

Every case, and what the design does

The Dashboard uses variant A, three statements with ready questions, and the depth lives in the AiMY conversation. This sheet walks through the situations a QA manager can land in, from the first visit to a filter change mid-conversation, and shows the design's answer to each. Every mock is live and uses illustrative data.

The happy path

Cases 1 to 6 are one journey: notice, ask, read the evidence, see where the research is, act, and come back later.

Case 1

First visit: notice what recurs

When

A QA manager opens the Dashboard for Product A. They have not asked anything yet.

What the design does

The insights card leads with one conclusion, then the three issues that matter most this period, each with its count, work state and a question ready to send. The scope line says exactly which calls this is based on.

Try it: click a question chip.

Case 2

Investigate one issue

When

The manager clicks "Why do customers find setup unclear?".

What the design does

The conversation opens over the Dashboard with the question already sent. AiMY answers in one message: Based on chips, From your calls with quotes that open the call, External research with its source and date, and the Suggested next step in AiMY blue with its own basis line.

Try it: open a call, dismiss the suggestion and undo it.

Case 3

Research is not available

When

Same question, but comparable-product research has not run for this issue.

What the design does

The research part says so in one sentence with the reason, and nothing is invented. The Based on chips lose the source chip. The suggestion stays, labelled as based on your calls only, and the knowledge action stays with it.

Case 4

Which customers keep repeating

When

The manager wants the pattern by customer rather than by issue.

What the design does

They ask in the chat, from the card chip or by typing. AiMY answers with a sentence and a short ranked list of business units with counts, each with a follow-up. No Group by control, no table on the page.

Case 5

Act: draft a knowledge article

When

The manager agrees with the suggestion and wants the article fixed.

What the design does

AiMY writes the draft into the thread as a reviewed action: the text, what it is based on, where it goes, and Accept, Edit, Reject. Accept sends it to the Knowledge review queue, the toast offers Undo, and the issue turns Drafted on the card.

Try it: accept, undo from the toast, or reject.

Case 6

Coming back a week later

When

The manager returns to the same issue after sending the draft.

What the design does

The card shows Drafted. Opening the chat continues the earlier thread instead of starting over: AiMY says what was sent, where it stands, and what changed since, with the next questions ready.

When the data is not what the user expects

Cases 7 to 12 are the states the happy path does not show. Each says what is missing and offers a way forward; none shows a blank card.

Case 7

No calls in this period

When

The selected period has no analyzed calls for Product A.

What the design does

The card says so, names the last analyzed call, and offers the two moves that usually fix it as questions: a longer period or a wider product scope.

Case 8

Nothing is recurring

When

Calls exist, but no issue crossed the recurring threshold.

What the design does

This is good news and looks like it. The card states the rule in plain words so the user knows what "recurring" means here, and offers to show the one-off problems instead.

Case 9

Only part of the calls are analyzed

When

Analysis is still running on 68 of 248 calls.

What the design does

The card carries a note with the exact numbers. In the chat, AiMY says the same thing in its Based on chips and its sentence, and promises to update the answer. The two never disagree.

Case 10

AiMY is still working

When

The question was sent and the answer has not arrived.

What the design does

The thread shows the typing indicator with one line about what AiMY is reading, so the wait is explained rather than silent.

Case 11

Filters changed during the conversation

When

The manager switches the product filter while the chat is open on a Product A issue.

What the design does

AiMY notices and says the issue is now outside the selection, then offers either to go back to Product A or to ask the same question for the new product. The page underneath already reflects the new filter.

Try it: choose "Back to Product A insights".

Case 12

A user limited to their own data

When

An agent whose scope is "Only my results" opens the Dashboard.

What the design does

Product insights are about customers, not about the agent, so the card does not switch to a personal view. It says so, points to My Profile for the agent's own feedback, and shows no team member names anywhere.

One rule across all twelve

The card and the conversation always say the same thing about the same data: the same counts, the same scope, the same state. If one of them cannot answer, it says why and offers a question, never a blank.

All figures, issues and customer names in the mocks are illustrative.
