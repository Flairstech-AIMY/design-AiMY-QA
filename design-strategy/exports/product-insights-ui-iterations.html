Product insights: six iterations, one mix, and where each goes

For the AiMY QA Dashboard: six ways to draw the Product insights card, each paired with a place on the Dashboard, and a seventh that mixes the recurring-issues tile (6) with the issue cards (4). They all use the Dashboard's own figures: 2,486 analyzed calls, 12 recurring issues taking 112 of 307 support hours (36%), led by Setup instructions unclear at 30 h. Click any issue to see what it would open in AiMY, and switch themes to check both.

Recommended: iteration 2 in the left column, with iteration 5 as the way in

Built: iteration 7, the mix, is what the Dashboard now ships, as its own section after the KPI row (slot D).

Today the card sits under Audit performance by product, so it reads as more audit data, and the right column runs far longer than the left. Product insights answers a different question, what customers keep calling about, and its action is a knowledge fix.

Placing it under Improvement opportunities makes the left column "what to fix": agents above, knowledge below. It also evens out the two columns. Iteration 2 is short enough for that slot and leads with the one decision that matters. Adding iteration 5's line to Since your last visit puts the costliest issue in front of the manager without moving the card up.
