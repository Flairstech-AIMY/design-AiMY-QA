Product Insights

Five ways to bring Product Insights into AiMY QA

Each option below is placed on the real Dashboard or Reviews page, with the product's own shell, AiMY conversation and components, so you can click through it as a user would. The feature follows the AiMY design system; nothing in the design system was changed to fit it. All figures are illustrative.

Every client contractAudit, Evaluation and Audit + Evaluation clients all see it.
Every roleQA managers, product owners and knowledge owners.
No "AiMY canvas" badgeThe conversation opens with the product's own "Based on" line.
Design system firstBuilt from existing components; the few gaps are marked as proposals.

What every option shares

  • The same AiMY answer: what customers report and what it costs, From your calls (quotes that play the call), Related articles (external ones say so, with source and date) and Suggested next step (what it is based on, and that it is untested).
  • The same knowledge draft: a reviewed action in the product's decision zone, Accept, Edit first or Reject. Accepting sends it to the knowledge review queue and the issue moves to Staged everywhere it is listed. Nothing publishes without a Knowledge Manager.
  • Ranked by support time, not count: Invoice (97 calls, 18 h) outranks Login (142 calls, 15 h). The bar in each row is its share of support time, in one neutral colour.
  • A scope sentence that reads the real filter row, and says when call length does not apply (Interaction: Tickets).
  • Typed questions work: asking "what are customers calling about" in the Ask AiMY bar reaches the same answers.
A

A card on the Dashboard, detail in AiMY

Recommended

A Product insights card under Audit performance by product, in that card's own anatomy: scope, cost pills, the three costliest issues, AiMY's line, and two action rows. Every row asks AiMY about that issue.

Open the live mockTry: click "Setup instructions unclear", then "Draft a knowledge article", then Accept.

Dashboard blocks: no change. The AiMY Detected strip is removed; its one line repeats what Improvement opportunities already lists. Evaluation clients get the card after their KPI row.

What works

  • Briefing-to-Canvas as the doctrine describes it: brief on the page, depth in AiMY.
  • The cost is visible without asking: 307 h of support, 36% on recurring issues.
  • Work state on every row, and it moves when a decision is made.

What it costs

  • The right column becomes longer than the left.
  • Customer issues sit next to agent-quality cards; the card's title has to carry the boundary.
  • Only three issues on the page; the rest are one question away.
B

Folded into blocks that already exist

No new block. Since your last visit gains one sentence and one question, and the costliest issue joins Improvement opportunities as a card.

Open the live mockJump to the cardSee the briefing's fourth button and the "Customers: setup instructions unclear" card.

Dashboard blocks: no change.

What works

  • Nothing new to learn and no layout change.
  • The briefing names the issue the moment the page opens.

What it costs

  • A customer issue ranked among audit KPIs reads as an agent failure, which breaks the audit and evaluation boundary in the README.
  • The briefing goes to four buttons.
  • One issue is visible; the other eleven and their cost are not.
C

AiMY only

Nothing on the Dashboard. A finding in the AiMY insights rail, a nudge above the Ask AiMY bar (the conversation-resume pill's component), and suggested questions when the conversation opens empty.

Open the live mockTry: the "New · 12 issues customers keep calling about" pill above the bar.

Dashboard blocks: no change.

What works

  • Cheapest to build; no layout risk.
  • The conversation carries the full depth, ranked list included.

What it costs

  • Easy to miss: a rail line and a pill compete with everything else.
  • Nothing on the page holds the work state, so a drafted fix has nowhere to show it moved.
  • The nudge and the "Continue" pill share the same spot.
D

A page of its own, with a Dashboard teaser

Customer issues joins the rail after Reviews. Four stat cards, Issues or Knowledge drafts, group by issue, client or product, and an issue list with its detail beside it. The Dashboard gets one line in the Detected strip's component.

Open the live mockSee the Dashboard teaserTry: Group by Client, and the Knowledge drafts tab.

Dashboard blocks: one more (the teaser line), unless it replaces the Detected strip as in A.

What works

  • Room to compare many issues, by client or product.
  • A real knowledge review queue, the one structured workflow the feature needs.
  • Product and knowledge owners get a page that is theirs.

What it costs

  • The doctrine allows a new page only when AiMY or an existing workflow cannot carry it; this has to be argued.
  • A seventh rail item.
  • The most to build and keep in step with the Dashboard.
E

An Issues tab in Reviews

A fifth tab after Calls, Tickets, Disputes and Agents, in Reviews' own table. A row asks AiMY about the issue; the rail's Reviews section gains a finding.

Open the live mockTry: a row, then the draft. Reviews' decision handler is HR-only, so the mock resolves the draft itself.

Dashboard blocks: no change.

What works

  • A table people already use, next to the calls the quotes come from.
  • No Dashboard change.

What it costs

  • Reviews is where agents are judged; customer issues there read as agent problems.
  • Reviews' filters (agent, status, sentiment) do not apply to issues.
  • Product owners would not look for it in Reviews.

Recommendation

  1. Ship A first. It keeps the Dashboard at its block budget, shows the cost without a question, and uses the conversation for depth as the doctrine intends.
  2. Take two things from D later: the knowledge review queue (a structured workflow AiMY should hand off to) and grouping by client or product. Give them a page only if testing shows managers need to compare many issues at once.
  3. Avoid B and E. Both put customer issues where agents are judged.

Proposed for the design system (marked PROPOSED in product-insights/pi.css): the scope sentence, the section label inside an answer, the customer quote that plays the call, the related-article row, and the issue row's meta line. Everything else is an existing component.

Rebuild after the product pages change: node design-strategy/product-insights/build.mjs. Switch options inside a mock from Demo controls (bottom right).
